# Entry 7
##### 5/25/26

### Content
Every student in Sep10 made a presentation about their freedom project. which was theirn website  about the past and future technologies within the Physical therapy treatments for someone with cerebral palsy! 
### EDP
I started my presentation by a statistical question about the benefit of physical therapy for individuals with Cp. So to keep my audience wondering what benefits physical therapy does or doesn't have for those with cp. Then I proceeded with describing the process of how I got my information for past technologies. I stated that I got them from w3schools and past experiences. For Future technologies I stated the technologies that I wish I had at my disposal to help me. For the product I showed off my website which I later edited a lot after the in class presentation for the expo as well as how my tool CSS Flexbox helped with organizing the cards and boxes of my website.  

## Skills 
## Attention to detail
I learned to pay attention to small details when creating the slides and giving this presentation of what I wrote in the presentation slides, and how I said them in the presentation verses how I wanted to say the slides out loud.

## Communication 
Giving my presentation in class vs giving it to six  people at the expo I learned how to communicate a message to a big crowd versus one person through an elevator pitch. Learning how to give a presentation in one minute or less.

## Takeaways
 Some takeaways from my presentation. is to take it slow so that I don't say something twice or stutter when speaking. For an elevator pitch maybe talking with my hands helps more than I thought. But most of all I'm happy that I got to present my disability in a way that makes me feel empowered about it and not down upon it. Although there will be its moments. But this website reminds me of them. I have a purpose in this world.
